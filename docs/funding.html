Magic Tray · support

Help fund a Magic Mouse driver checked for Windows 10 and 11

Magic Tray is free and always will be, and so is the driver. Nobody has paid for Microsoft's check on that driver, the software Windows uses to talk to your mouse.

What would the money buy?

Paying for Microsoft's check on the Magic Mouse driver removes the security warning when you install it, and removes the Windows startup setting you currently have to change to make the 2024 mouse scroll. It would work for everyone, not only donors. Magic Tray itself stays free regardless.

Free ways to help

Most people won't pay a penny, and these free actions help as much as money. Star Magic Tray and the 2024 mouse fix on GitHub. Tell us your Apple device works so it joins the tested list. Test a Magic Keyboard or a Magic Trackpad nobody has confirmed.

Devices that need a tester

Only three Apple devices have ever been confirmed by a real tester: the Magic Mouse 2024 (0323), the Magic Mouse v1 (030D) and the 2011 Apple Wireless Keyboard (0239). Every Magic Keyboard and every Magic Trackpad should work, but nobody has confirmed one yet.
