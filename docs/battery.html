See your Apple mouse, keyboard and trackpad battery on Windows 10 and 11

Windows shows no battery percent for these devices. Magic Tray puts one beside your clock, free.

Which devices show a battery percentage?

Every Apple Magic Mouse, Magic Keyboard and Magic Trackpad Magic Tray recognises. A keyboard needs a one-time unlock first. Nothing else needs anything extra.

Low battery warnings

Magic Tray warns you at 10, 5 or 1 percent. Pick the level under Low battery alert in the tray menu, or turn it off. Every alert level.

No percent showing?

Wake the device and pick Refresh Now. Still blank: the icon is behind the taskbar's ^ arrow, a keyboard has not had its unlock, or Magic Tray does not know that model. Scrolling is a separate fix.

Common questions

Can I see my Apple keyboard's battery percent on Windows?

Yes, after a one-time unlock you run from the Magic Tray menu. Windows keeps its own keyboard driver. Only the 2011 Apple Wireless Keyboard is confirmed so far.

How do I check my Apple Magic Trackpad battery on Windows?

Hover the battery icon beside your clock. Nothing extra to do. Magic Trackpad 2 (0265), the 2024 trackpad (0324) and the AA-battery one (030E) should all report a percent, but nobody has confirmed one yet.
